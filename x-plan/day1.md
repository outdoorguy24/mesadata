## 2. Three standalone posts for day 1

Suggested order: reply to threads first (see below), then post #1 midday, #2 late afternoon, #3 tomorrow morning if you'd rather space them.

**Post 1: Rights first: the pre-call checklist (law, accounting, insurance, manufacturing)** (251 chars)

> Before a call with a data buyer, check four things:
> 
> 1. Customer contracts: who owns what you generate?
> 2. Employee agreements
> 3. Regulated or privileged data
> 4. Any past exclusive that already limits you
> 
> Can't answer one? You don't have a price yet.

**Post 2: License vs one-time sale** (245 chars)

> A flat fee for your records is a snapshot sale. A license can have a term, a field of use, and the right to license again.
> 
> Exclusivity changes the price and what a future buyer of your company inherits.
> 
> Ask for the structure before the number.

**Post 3: De-identification and audit rights** (258 chars)

> "We de-identify it" is a claim, not a control.
> 
> Ask: who does it, how is it tested, what gets missed, who holds the raw files and for how long, and can you audit it?
> 
> For law, insurance and healthcare firms, that answer decides whether there's a deal at all.
