Questions

Plain answers.

How is the range calculated?

From your answers: U.S. full-time headcount, years of history, the systems you run, the kinds of work they record, your industry and whether the old data is still reachable. The formula is published in the page source. It is illustrative, not an offer. Final price is set by buyers after review.

Do I have to share any files?

No. The estimate and the first review need no files. If a buyer is interested later, any data handling is agreed in writing first.

Why 100 or more employees?

Buyers want depth: many people, many years, consistent systems. That usually means 100+ staff. Companies with 50 to 99 can still be reviewed but are a limited fit. Smaller companies can join the waitlist.

What happens after I request a review?

We look at your company and reply by email with whether there is a real fit and what the next step would be. Nothing goes to a buyer without your OK.

Can I come back later?

Yes. Answers are saved in this browser only, so you can pick up where you left off.
